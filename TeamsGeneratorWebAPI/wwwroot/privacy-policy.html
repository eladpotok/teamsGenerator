Teamify Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how eladpeleg ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Teamify mobile application and related services.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, authentication provider, and the account identifier created by Firebase Authentication.
  • Squad and matchday information: player names, skill ratings, positions, goalkeeper status, attendance, team assignments, team names, user-entered match locations, scores, goals, assists, substitutions, match history, and app settings.
  • Shared matchday information: information included in scoreboards, roster images, team images, or sharing links that you choose to create.
  • Diagnostics and usage information: request, feature-usage, error, browser, device, and approximate network information processed through application telemetry.

Teamify does not request precise device location. A match location entered in Teamify is text provided by the user.

How we use information

  • Authenticate users and maintain their accounts.
  • Save squads, settings, matchdays, scoreboards, and match history.
  • Generate and improve balanced teams, including optional chemistry calculations based on match history.
  • Create images, sharing links, and AI-assisted match summaries when requested.
  • Secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve Teamify.

AI-assisted features

When you explicitly request an AI summary, relevant match facts such as player names, goals, assists, scores, and match statistics are sent to our configured Microsoft Azure OpenAI service to generate the response. Account passwords are never sent to the AI service.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only as necessary to provide Teamify, when you choose to share it, when required by law, or to protect users and the service. Our principal service providers are:

  • Google Firebase: account authentication and sign-in services.
  • Microsoft Azure: API hosting, data storage, application telemetry, and AI processing.
  • Google Play Services: distribution and platform services on Android devices.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms and security obligations.

Data retention and deletion

Account and matchday information is retained while your Teamify account is active and as needed to operate the service. Diagnostic records and backups may remain for a limited period after deletion for security, recovery, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

You may request deletion of your Teamify account and associated data from inside the app or through our public Account Deletion page. Verified requests are normally completed within 30 days. Residual backup copies may take up to 90 days to expire.

Security

We use authentication, HTTPS network encryption, access controls, and managed cloud infrastructure to protect information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may update squad and configuration information in the app, choose whether to create sharing links or AI summaries, sign out, and request account deletion. You may contact us to ask about your information or a deletion request.

Children's privacy

Teamify is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can delete it.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We rely on their applicable contractual and legal safeguards for international data transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Teamify or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version.
