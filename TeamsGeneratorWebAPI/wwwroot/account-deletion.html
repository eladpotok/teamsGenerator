Delete Your Teamify Account

This page is provided by eladpeleg for users of the Teamify app.

Request account deletion

  1. Send the request from the email address associated with your Teamify account.
  2. Use the subject Teamify account deletion request.
  3. Include the display name used in Teamify. Do not send your password.
Email deletion request

What will be deleted

After verification, we delete the Teamify authentication account and associated squad, player, settings, team, scoreboard, match history, and sharing data under our control.

Time required

Verified requests are normally completed within 30 days. Limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Residual backup copies may take up to 90 days to expire.

For more information, read the Teamify Privacy Policy.
